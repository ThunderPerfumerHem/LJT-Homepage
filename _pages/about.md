# Junteng Liu

First-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, pursuing a PhD in Computer Science (2024–Present) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). Graduated from the Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.

## Academic Background

- **Bachelor (2020–2024)** at the Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).
- **PhD in Computer Science (2024–Present)** at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), under the supervision of Professor Junxian He.
- Received the **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship** at the Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

## Research Experience

Research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

- **Research Intern at MINIMAX** (February 2025 – Present)
- **Research Intern at Tencent WXG** (June 2024 – September 2024)
- **Research Intern at Shanghai AI Lab** (June 2023 – December 2023)

PhD supervisor: Professor Junxian He, who leads the HKUST NLP Group.

## Skills

- Natural Language Processing
- Machine Learning
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
